Retail Business Questions — Day 3
Question	Expected answer
1. What are the total net sales by store?	Store ID and total net sales
2. Which five products generated the highest net sales?	Product ID, name, total net sales and rank
3. What is the daily sales trend?	Date and daily net sales
4. Which products have low closing inventory?	Store ID, product ID and latest closing stock
5. Which stores experienced stockouts?	Store ID and number of store-product-days with zero closing stock
